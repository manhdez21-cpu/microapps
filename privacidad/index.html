Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026.

1. Who we are

Chispatools is a personal tool operated by one person (the "Operator"). It is not offered to the public. Contact: [TU CORREO].

2. Data we access

When the Operator connects a TikTok account through Login Kit, the tool receives the account's Open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic) and an access token that allows uploading videos (scope video.upload, and video.publish if enabled). The tool does not read private messages, followers, or other users' data.

3. How we use it

Only to upload videos created by the Operator to the Operator's own account, and to show the connection status in the Operator's dashboard.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted on the Operator's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to third parties other than TikTok's own API.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account is connected. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services), and the tool deletes the stored token on disconnect. To request deletion of any data, write to chispatools.contacto@gmailcom.

6. Children

The tool is not directed to children and does not collect data from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
